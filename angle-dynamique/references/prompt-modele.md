# Modèle de prompt (structure qui a marché sur Seedance)

À remplir pour chaque partie de 2 à 4 plans (10 à 12 s). Écris le prompt en anglais : les outils le suivent mieux. Garde les blocs dans cet ordre. Joins une fiche de référence par personnage, objet ou décor qui revient, et cite-les par leur numéro d'image.

```
GLOBAL STYLE
Fake amateur footage, mid-2000s, filmed by [qui filme] with a cheap [GoPro HERO / consumer camcorder]. [Mots du look A ou B.] Slight handheld shake at all times. Nothing is ever framed face-on: always from an angle, from the side or slightly from behind, crooked framing. Never cinematic, never smooth, never clean.

SCENE
[L'histoire en 2 phrases : le truc absurde, filmé comme si c'était vrai. Le personnage principal reste calme.]

CHARACTERS
- [Personnage 1] = image 1. [Ce qui ne change jamais : tenue, accessoires, où sont ses lunettes…]
- [Personnage 2 / animal / véhicule] = image 2. [« always visible », « pulled by its reindeer »…]
- [Témoin] = image 3. [Sa réaction : terrified, frozen, eyes wide.]

LOCATION
[Le décor] = image 4. [Ce qui doit rester identique d'un plan à l'autre. Ce qu'il ne faut pas montrer.]

FIRST FRAME
[La toute première image, cadrage compris.]

SHOT 1 (0-2.5s) - [Nom du mouvement]. [Ce qui se passe, en une action. Comment se fait le mouvement. Comment le plan se termine : hard cut / cut inside the blur.]

SHOT 2 (2.5-5s) - [Nom du mouvement]. [...]

SHOT 3 (5-7.5s) - [Nom du mouvement]. [...]

SHOT 4 (7.5-10s) - [Nom du mouvement]. [...]

OPTICS
[wide-angle distortion / full digital zoom / focus hunts / on-camera flash]. Crash zooms are real optical zooms: the camera does not move forward.

PHYSICS
[Gravité, vitesse, ce qui flotte, ce qui tombe.]

LIGHTING
[Lumière moche et réaliste : flash de l'appareil, soleil dur, néon plat, noirs bouchés.]

AUDIO
[Sons réels : moteurs, coups, cris, souffle.] No music. No text, no logo.
```

## Exemple rempli (partie 1 de la pub du père Noël)

```
GLOBAL STYLE
Fake amateur footage, mid-2000s, filmed by an astronaut with a cheap GoPro HERO from a space station. Wide-angle distortion, soft compressed low-res image, crushed blacks, slight handheld shake at all times. Nothing is ever framed face-on. Never cinematic, never smooth.

SCENE
A gangsta Santa drives a rocket sleigh over Earth in winter, parks next to the station and covers it with Christmas garlands. The astronaut films it, terrified. Santa stays calm, chewing gum.

CHARACTERS
- Santa = image 1: gold chains, small round sunglasses ON HIS EYES, never on the hat.
- Rocket sleigh = image 2, pulled by its reindeer, always visible in front of the sleigh.
- Astronaut = image 3: light blue polo shirt, no spacesuit.

LOCATION
Space station = image 4, seen from one angle only, never as a straight symmetrical cross. Earth = image 5, below, black space around.

FIRST FRAME
Tiny sleigh far away over Earth, seen at an angle from the station.

SHOT 1 (0-3s) - Crash zoom + whip pan. The astronaut zooms in violently on Santa: real optical zoom, the camera does not move forward, in 6 frames the frame is a tight, blurry close-up, the image shakes when the zoom stops. Sudden whip pan to the left, heavy streaky blur, hard cut inside the blur.

SHOT 2 (3-5.5s) - Long lens, handheld. Filmed from far away with full digital zoom, Santa parks next to the station, seen from the side and slightly from behind, and presses a small remote without looking. Focus hunts once. Hard cut.

SHOT 3 (5.5-8s) - Follow tilt + punch-in. Garlands shoot out from under the sleigh, the camera tilts down fast to follow them, losing framing, then a hard punch-in on the station hull covered in garlands.

SHOT 4 (8-10s) - Wide-angle close-up, handheld. The astronaut in profile against the porthole, eyes wide, hands flat on the glass, recoiling. On-camera light flattens his face.

AUDIO
Rocket engine roar, garlands whooshing, panicked breathing. No music. No text, no logo.
```
